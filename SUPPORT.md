# Support

Supported questions and reproducible defects are welcome through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kirklasalle/SpaceEntropyCompression/issues).
Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kirklasalle/SpaceEntropyCompression/discussions)
for open-ended usage and research conversation.

Before filing a bug:

```text
emrf doctor --report
emrf kb validate
```

Review the generated report before attaching it. It excludes known credential
and personal-path surfaces, but the reporter remains responsible for the
content they share. Never upload tokens, private archives, unpublished
personal data, or observations whose licence forbids redistribution.

Scientific questions must identify evidence class, units, assumptions, data
provenance, and regime of validity. Software success is not physical evidence.
