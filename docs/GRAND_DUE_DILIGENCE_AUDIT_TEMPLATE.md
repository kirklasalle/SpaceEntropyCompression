# PART I: THE GRAND COVENANT

The Grand Covenant represents the sacred moral and philosophical compact between the scientific investigator, the software architect, the global scientific community, and the living biosphere. Any project adopting this framework formally binds itself to the following four Articles:

3. **Adversarial Self-Challenge:** The software must actively attempt to break and falsify the theory. A system that cannot aggressively reject unphysical data has no authority to validate physical data.

## Section 1: Fundamental Physics Directives (Domain P)

* **Directive P-1: The Immutable Relativistic Baseline**  
  General Relativity (GR) is treated as the established, empirical gold standard. Every modified or emergent theory must:
  1. Implement an exact, standard GR baseline against which all claims are benchmarked.
  2. Prove that in regimes of established experimental precision (e.g., Solar System, binary pulsars, Sgr A* relativistic orbits), the theory reduces to GR to within known empirical limits ($|\Delta a| / a \le 10^{-5}$ to $10^{-14}$).

* **Directive P-2: Strict Falsifiability & The Pre-Registered Bifurcation Protocol**  
  Every speculative model must declare explicit, quantitative criteria for its own disproof prior to fitting data:
  * **Branch A (Geometric Collapse):** If the model parameters reduce to coordinate or metric invariants of standard General Relativity, the framework must formally declare Branch A, acknowledging that no novel fifth force exists.
  * **Branch B (Novel Physics):** Novel physical degrees of freedom may only be claimed if supported by decisive statistical model selection (e.g., $\Delta\text{BIC} \le -10.0$) across simultaneous, multi-target, cluster-wide joint fits, while ruling out observational selection bias.

* **Directive P-3: Dimensional and Ontological Consistency**  
  Physical quantities must respect strict dimensional ontology:
  1. Spatial manifolds $\mathcal{M}^D$ consist of spatial coordinates $X = (x, y, z, d_0, d_1, \dots)$.
  2. Coordinate time $t$ tracks temporal duration and dynamical evolution; time must never be lazily conflated with spatial axes without formal pseudo-Riemannian signature treatment.
  3. Entropy $S(X,t)$ is an organizational, thermodynamic state variable or functional; it must never be treated as an orthogonal geometric spatial coordinate without explicit thermodynamic equation-of-state coupling.

* **Directive P-4: Conservation Laws, Invariance & Stress-Energy Balance**  
  Any proposed field equation or emergent gravity framework must satisfy:
  1. Coordinate independence (Diffeomorphism invariance).
  2. Local conservation of energy-momentum: $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$.
  3. Consistency with the contracted Bianchi identities: $\nabla_\mu G^{\mu\nu} = 0$.

* **Directive P-5: Hamiltonian Stability, Ghost Absence & Hyperbolic Causality**  
  To prevent unphysical quantum decay or acausal paradoxes, the field Lagrangian must undergo strict perturbation auditing:
  1. **Ostrogradsky Ghost Freedom:** Field equations of motion must be strictly of second order or lower ($\le 2$nd order time derivatives).
  2. **Kinetic Positivity:** The kinetic matrix $A(a)$ must be strictly positive-definite ($A > 0$), ensuring the Hamiltonian energy spectrum is bounded from below.
  3. **Subluminal Hyperbolicity:** Perturbation sound speeds must be strictly real, positive, and subluminal ($0 < c_s \le c$), preventing tachyon instabilities, gradient catastrophes, and superluminal causality violations.

* **Directive P-6: Multi-Regime Cross-Confrontation**  
  A theory cannot validate itself in a single observational regime and declare victory. It must survive confrontation across at least **six distinct physical scales**:
  1. Strong-field relativistic regime ($a \gg a_0$, near black holes).
  2. Solar system precision screening regime ($10^{-6} < a < 10^2\text{ m/s}^2$).
  3. Weak-field galactic rotation regime ($a \ll a_0$, SPARC database).
  4. Relativistic lensing and null-geodesic regime (HST/SLACS lenses, unit slip $\eta = 1$).
  5. Dynamic cosmological expansion ($z \le 2.5$, Type Ia SNe & BAO).
  6. Early-universe perturbation & acoustic scale ($z \sim 1100$, CMB acoustic peaks).

* **Directive S-1: Deterministic Reproducibility & Pipeline Automation**  
  1. Every result, table, figure, and statistical metric must be generated through fully automated, non-interactive CLI scripts.
  2. Random number generators must use fixed, documented seeds.
  3. Raw observational datasets must be immutably preserved in clean, standard CSV/FITS formats with complete provenance documentation.

* **Directive S-2: Continuous Integration & Automated Unit Test Coverage**  
  1. The codebase must maintain comprehensive automated unit tests covering 100% of physical equations, coordinate projections, numerical integrators, and statistical comparison routines.
  2. Every commit must pass all tests with zero failures prior to merging or publishing.
  3. Execution of the complete test suite must complete within reasonable time limits ($< 30$ seconds) to enable friction-free developer verification.

* **Directive S-3: Numerical Stability, Error Propagation & Convergence**  
  1. All numerical integrators (e.g., Runge-Kutta, symplectic integrators) must undergo step-size convergence audits ($\Delta t \to \Delta t / 2$) to prove stability.
  2. Error propagation from observational uncertainties must be tracked using formal covariance matrices or Monte Carlo error injections.
  3. Algorithms must guard against catastrophic numerical cancellation, floating-point overflows, and ill-conditioned matrix inversions.

* **Directive S-4: Adversarial Blind Falsification Gauntlets**  
  1. The software suite must contain automated **Adversarial Blind Engines** that feed synthetic, non-physical datasets (e.g., inverted anti-gravity $V \propto -r$, discontinuous Heaviside steps, pure Gaussian white noise) into the fitting pipeline.
  2. The software must successfully reject 100% of unphysical datasets with decisive statistical rejection ($\chi^2_{\text{red}} \gg 2.0$, $p < 10^{-6}$), proving that the framework is a constrained physical model, not an over-parameterized curve-fitting spline.

* **Directive S-5: Decoupled Architecture & Separation of Concerns**

