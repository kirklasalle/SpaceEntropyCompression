## 2. The Grand Covenant

The Grand Covenant represents the moral and philosophical compact between the scientific investigator, the software architect, the broader open-source community, and the living biosphere.

### Article I: The Covenant of Truth and Scientific Humility
1. **Nature as Supreme Arbiter:** The investigator acknowledges that nature owes no allegiance to human aesthetic preferences, mathematical elegance, or personal ambition. Observational data is the supreme and unappealable court of arbitration.
2. **Rejection of Vanity & Confirmation Bias:** The investigator shall never protect a beloved hypothesis through ad-hoc parameters, suppressed counter-evidence, selective data filtering (cherry-picking), or p-hacking.
3. **The Nobility of Disproof and Correspondence:** The alignment of a speculative hypothesis with an established baseline (such as General Relativity) is celebrated as a definitive scientific achievement and proof of correspondence, not mourned as a defeat. Truth is the only victory.

### Article II: The Biosphere and Life Protection Covenant
1. **Reverence for All Sentient Life:** Scientific investigation and computational technologies must actively honor, protect, and respect the human species and all animal species inhabiting our biosphere.
2. **Prevention of Ecological & Energetic Catastrophe:** Theoretical inquiries that explore novel energetic densities, metric distortions, or gravitational dynamics must establish rigorous theoretical bounds proving that proposed mechanisms cannot trigger runaway vacuum instabilities, destructive field decays, or ecological harm.
3. **Good Works as Mandatory Purpose:** The outputs of this research—whether open-source code, educational visualizers, or physical preprints—shall be dedicated to benevolent enlightenment, peaceful knowledge dissemination, environmental preservation, and the collective flourishing of planetary life.

### Article III: The Covenant of Computational Integrity
1. **Zero-Deceit Codebase:** Every numerical algorithm, integrator, regression engine, and statistical solver must reflect honest arithmetic. No hardcoded fudge factors, simulated noise masks, or artificial smoothing shall ever be introduced to manufacture the appearance of agreement with data.
2. **Complete Deterministic Reproducibility:** Every figure, table, metric, and reduced $\chi^2$ published to the world must be completely reproducible from raw, open observational tables using a single, verifiable automated command.
3. **Adversarial Self-Challenge:** The software must actively attempt to break and falsify the theory. A system that cannot aggressively reject unphysical data has no authority to validate physical data.

### Article IV: The Covenant of Generational Stewardship
1. **Democratization of Knowledge:** High-level scientific inquiry must not be hoarded behind paywalls or restricted to academic gatekeepers. Tools, documentation, and data must be curated with clarity so that practicing physicists, independent scholars, students, and hobbyists alike can inspect and build upon the work.
2. **Archival Permanence:** Code and data must be preserved in permanent, immutable, cryptographic repositories (e.g., Zenodo DOIs, Git repositories with SHA-256 integrity proofs) to ensure that the scientific record remains unbroken for future generations.

## 3. Planetary Impact, Ecological Stewardship & Life Safety (Domain E)

* **Directive E-1: Planetary Safety and Dual-Use Hazard Audit**  
  The research team assesses whether mathematical or computational models could be weaponized or leveraged to design destructive mechanisms. If speculative modifications indicate potential high-energy field collapse, containment protocols and safety margins must be formally derived before physical experimentation is proposed.
* **Directive E-2: Computational Energy and Ecological Efficiency**  
  In consideration of planetary energy consumption and carbon impact, algorithms are optimized for algorithmic efficiency ($O(N)$ or $O(N \log N)$) rather than brute-force computational waste. All test suites and interactive solvers execute locally on standard hardware in under 6 seconds.
* **Directive E-3: Biocentric Reverence and Non-Human Life Preservation**  
  Technological applications arising from the physical theory must never contaminate natural ecosystems, disrupt avian or marine navigation systems (e.g., via electromagnetic or gravitational field distortion), or degrade animal habitats. Research is dedicated to the enhancement and preservation of biodiversity across all Earth species.
* **Directive E-4: Open Science, Transparency & Anti-Hype Dissemination**  
  Press releases, public summaries, and repository descriptions must strictly adhere to audited empirical facts. Sensationalist claims (e.g., "Einstein Proved Wrong!", "Anti-Gravity Discovered!") are strictly forbidden. All preprints, documentation, and source code are released under permissive open-source licenses with permanent cryptographic hashes.
