# Physics scope

EMRF studies whether specified geometric and thermodynamic descriptions can
provide useful, falsifiable mappings across gravitational regimes.

The recorded phenomenological mapping is:

```text
M(X,t) = k [C(X,t) / C0]^alpha
```

Its physical meaning depends on explicit definitions of `C`, `M`, units,
observers, dynamics, and validity conditions. Reproducing a standard identity
inside general relativity is a consistency result, not a new derivation of
matter or the full Einstein dynamics.

Entropy is a thermodynamic state variable, not an extra spatial coordinate.
Two candidate programs run concurrently. Candidate A re-describes existing
geometry and energy organization inside general relativity; Candidate B adds a
separately budgeted exchange sector, tested so far as a restricted
extra-dimension Higgs portal. Both remain research programs, not confirmed
replacements for general relativity or dark matter. B's public-data tests give
bounds on its new coupling, not evidence for it. A's one data test, of an
assistant-proposed operationalization (M = observed baryons), rejected that
operationalization on SPARC; Kirk's choice of C and M is still pending.
